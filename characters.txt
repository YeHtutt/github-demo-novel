===========
Characters
===========

Nick Carraway – A thoughtful and honest young man who moves to New York and 
                becomes Catsby’s neighbor. He is the narrator of the story.

Tom Buchanan – Daisy’s wealthy and arrogant husband. 
               He is aggressive, selfish, and comes from an old rich family.

Daisy Buchanan – A beautiful and charming woman who is married to Tom. 
                She is emotional but also careless and materialistic.
                
Jordan Baker – A confident and independent professional golfer. 
                She is calm, modern, and sometimes dishonest.

Myrtle Wilson – Myrtle is Tom’s mistress. She wants a richer and more exciting life and tries to act sophisticated, 
                but she is unhappy and trapped in her marriage.

George Wilson – George is Myrtle’s husband. He owns a small garage in the Valley of Ashes and appears tired, poor, and powerless.

Catherine – Myrtle’s sister. She is talkative and enjoys gossiping during the apartment party in New York.
