Chapter 1

1. Introduction of Nick Carraway
Nick tells the story from his perspective.
He moves from the Midwest to New York to work in the bond business.
He rents a small house in West Egg.

2. Introduction of Gatsby’s Mansion
Nick’s neighbor is Jay Gatsby.
Gatsby owns a huge and luxurious mansion.
Nick notices Gatsby standing outside and looking at a green light across the bay.

3. Visit to Tom and Daisy Buchanan
Nick visits his cousin Daisy and her husband Tom in East Egg.
Their house is elegant and expensive.
Jordan Baker is also visiting them.

4. Tom Buchanan’s Personality
Tom is strong, rich, and arrogant.
He behaves aggressively and likes to control others.
It becomes clear that he is having an affair.

5. Daisy Buchanan’s Character
Daisy appears charming and emotional.
She seems unhappy in her marriage.
She speaks in a playful but sometimes sad way.

6. Jordan Baker
Jordan is calm, confident, and fashionable.
Nick finds her interesting and attractive.

7. Important Themes Introduced
Wealth and social class
The difference between East Egg and West Egg
Love and marriage problems
The mystery surrounding Gatsby

8. Ending of the Chapter
Nick returns home.
He sees Gatsby reaching toward the green light across the water.
The green light becomes an important symbol of hope and dreams.
