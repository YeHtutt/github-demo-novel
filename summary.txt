The Great Gatsby is a novel about wealth, love, and the American Dream in the 1920s. The story is told by Nick Carraway, who moves to New York and meets the mysterious 
millionaire Jay Gatsby. Gatsby throws huge parties because he hopes to reunite with his former love, Daisy Buchanan, 
who is now married to Tom Buchanan. As Gatsby tries to win Daisy back, the story ends in tragedy and shows the emptiness behind wealth and social status.
